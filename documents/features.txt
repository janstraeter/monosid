monosid Features
----------------

Voices (x3)
----------------
- On/Off (Boolean)
- Waveform (Triangle, Pulse, Sawtooth und Noise) (Auswahl)
- Pulse-Width (12 Bit)
- ADSR (Attack, Decay, Sustain, Release jeweils 4 Bit)
- Sync (Boolean)
- Ring-Mod (Boolean)

Filter
-----------------
- Cutoff-Frequency (11 Bit)
- Resonance (4 Bit)
- Voice 1-3 (jeweils Boolean)
- Low-Pass, High-Pass, Bandwidth (jeweils Boolean)

Main
-----------------
- Master-Volume (4 Bit)

[LFO
-----------------
- Waveform (Triangle, Square) (Auswahl)
- LFO Rate (8 Bit?)
]
